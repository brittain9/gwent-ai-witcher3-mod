# Post-game review v2

After the match, submit up to three short reports using decision IDs you received.
A report must attach to a player decision returned to you. For an opponent
event, attach to your next returned decision and name the opponent event in the
report text.

Use labels own-mistake, opponent-weakness-candidate, or interface-issue. Submit
only claims supported by a specific observed decision. No report is preferable
to an invented issue.

Each report states: the observed event; a legal alternative if known; why the
alternative might matter; uncertainty; and how replay or a controlled comparison
could test the claim. Separate known scores from predicted outcomes. A loss does
not prove a mistake, and a win does not prove expert play. All reports are
unvalidated hypotheses, not training labels or search value targets.
