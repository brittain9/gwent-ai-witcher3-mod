All 13 collected games completed and replayed successfully, covering 396 engine
actions. The three-model varied panel contains nine of those games. It supports testing the
connector and finding decision examples; it is too small to rank model strength.

| Model | Reasoning | Wins–losses | Mean run time | Accepted player moves | Rejected player commands |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luna | xhigh | 2–1 | 5.6 min | 50 | 2 |
| Terra | xhigh | 3–0 | 2.0 min | 53 | 0 |
| Sol | high | 3–0 | 2.0 min | 50 | 0 |

Run time measures host startup to the written terminal result. It includes tool
calls, model scheduling and opponent computation, and is not model inference
latency. Player moves include opening and target choices. Luna's two rejected
commands were one authorization error and one malformed command; neither changed
game state. All three models recovered or completed with legal accepted actions.

the repaired starting-choice path, took 5.8 minutes, and belongs outside the model
comparison. One authorization error was recovered by refreshing state and
retrying. All 13 hosts were closed after collection.

Stock reproduces native behavior and accesses both hands for its potential
calculations. Per-action stock randomness was recorded but not paired across
models. Time-bounded search can also vary. These controls are sufficient for an
exploratory panel, not a controlled strength claim. See the
[readiness review](readiness-review.md) for the full limits.

## Decision quality

Wins alone do not qualify demonstrations. Luna won two varied games while still
making a Tight Bond interpretation error. It also selected a ranged Horn while
its rationale described melee. The stronger models supplied plausible reviews,
but those reviews are not automatically correct either.

Two local Luna findings were checked against the engine:

1. **Pilot stock game, decision 4:** the opponent had passed at five points.
   Replaying the prefix showed Dethmold alone won 6–5, avoiding the extra Blue
   Stripes card spent by the player. This verifies a local resource-saving line,
   not its full-match value.
2. **Varied Skellige game, decision 22:** the selected Horn went to a ranged row
   containing only a hero and left the score at 50. Replaying the legal siege Horn
   instead raised the score to 74. This verifies an immediate 24-point difference;
   later opponent responses still determine match value.

Reports about early passing, delaying Scorch until a second Catapult appears,
and preserving cards remain hypotheses. Later knowledge of the opponent's hand
cannot by itself justify a label for an earlier decision.

Use Luna for diverse exploratory trajectories and interface checks. Use
Terra/Sol or search for targeted review, and verify proposed labels. This is a
workflow recommendation from observed errors, not proof that either stronger
model consistently plays optimally.

engine/model hashes, seeds, limits, results, timings, review associations and
replay status. Raw journals contain privileged state; live players only receive
their seat view. Reports remain separate and unvalidated. No automatic tool-access
audit or training admission has been performed.
